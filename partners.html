PRIVACY

What you do and don’t get

UERA runs entirely in the athlete’s browser. Nothing they answer is stored or sent anywhere, and that includes you. The link only chooses whose name and logo appear. You will not receive their answers, scores, or any tracking data.

If an athlete wants you to see their results, they can send you their PDF, or bring it to a session. That keeps the choice with them, and it is the same promise UERA makes to everyone who uses it.

HOW IT WORKS

From application to live link

  1. Buy a coach license above.
  2. Set up your branding. Right after you pay you land on a setup page. Enter your name, website, a short message to your athletes, and upload your logo (PNG, JPG or WebP, at least 200 px wide, under 300 KB). It is shown on a light tile, so a dark or full-color logo works best.
  3. It goes live in a minute or two. You get an email with your link and a private link for changing your branding later. Closed the page before finishing? The same setup link is in your email.
  4. It stays live while your license is active. If your subscription ends, your branding switches off automatically, and buying again switches it back on.

Your branding is checked automatically when you save it (https website, a real image, no links or claims to have built the tool in your message). I may still remove a branded link, for example if it could mislead athletes about who built the tool.
